# Blind 250-Trial Shiva Draft Stability Audit

250 deterministic-seed blind sensitivity trials. Each trial varies portfolio/efficiency/construction weights, injury adjustment, replacement level, empirical round-weight clipping, and structural QB/TE/K/DST rules within plausible ranges. Team/owner identities are not used until all scores, ranks, pairwise comparisons, and tiers are frozen. Consensus rank = median trial rank, then mean rank, then Top-25 frequency.

Within Top 50, create a new tier only when the higher adjacent draft beats the lower in at least 70% of the 250 blind trials.
